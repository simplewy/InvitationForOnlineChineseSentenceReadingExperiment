  • Please complete all tasks carefully. You may take breaks at any time, but we kindly ask you not to pause while a sentence is still on the screen, as this may affect the data. Instead, please feel free to rest while you are answering the comprehension questions.

  • Also, let us know your preferred payment method (preferably PayPal or Apple Pay) and your bank details, so we can arrange the compensation.
